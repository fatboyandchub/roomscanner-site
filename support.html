Support

Questions, problems or ideas: roomscanner@3dlibrary.app. Say which iPhone or iPad you have and what you were doing when something went wrong, and you'll hear back from a person.

Which devices can scan?

There are three ways to measure a room, and the app offers the ones your device can do.

  • Scan with LiDAR (Detect Walls) needs the LiDAR scanner: iPhone 12 Pro and later Pro and Pro Max models, and iPad Pro from 2020 on. On a device without it, Detect Walls shows greyed out with a note, and the other two ways are there.
  • Walk the corners (Detect Corners) works on any iPhone or iPad that runs the app. It uses the camera to find the floor; no LiDAR needed.
  • Draw the walls needs no camera at all: drag the corners into place and type the lengths.

The app needs iOS 26.1 or later (iPadOS 26.1 on an iPad). It's made for iPhone and iPad; it isn't offered on the Mac.

iCloud, on every device

Your projects are kept in your own iCloud and show up on every device signed in to the same Apple Account; the app needs no account of its own. If they aren't syncing, a card on the Projects screen says why. If it says to sign in, open the device's Settings, tap your name at the top and sign in to iCloud. If your iCloud is full, free some room or add storage, and the waiting changes go up by themselves. Back up to iCloud, in the app's Settings, turns the whole thing off or on.

The free month, cancelling and restoring

The first month is free. After it, the plan you picked starts and Apple charges it; the prices are on the home page. To change or cancel, open the device's Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Cancelling keeps the app open until the end of the time you've paid for.

New phone, or reinstalled the app? Sign in with the same Apple Account and tap Restore Purchases in the app's Settings.

Deleting your data

Delete All My Data, in the app's Settings, removes every project from your device and from your iCloud. Deleting the app removes the copy on the device; the iCloud copy stays until you remove it in the app or under iCloud storage in the device's Settings. The privacy policy says what the app stores and where.

Exporting the PDF

On a project, tap the page button, then Create PDF. Pick the rooms to include and, if you want them, a room's wall views. The preview shows the pages: the Home Inspect logo and the project's name at the top of each, the whole home's plan first, then each room. Tap Share to send it or save it.

Where are the photos and the address?

This app keeps to the inside of the home. There's no camera button, no photos and no address: nothing leaves your device but your own iCloud, the share sheet and the App Store.
