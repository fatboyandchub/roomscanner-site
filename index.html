Three ways to measure

Scan it, walk it, or draw it.

Pick the way that fits the room and the phone in your hand. Each one ends in the same plan, 3D model and wall views.

Scan with LiDAR

Hold the phone up and walk the room once, every corner, floor to ceiling. The walls, doors and windows come in on their own.

iPhone Pro · iPad Pro

Walk the corners

No LiDAR? Aim at each corner where the wall meets the floor and tap. Then the ceiling, and each door and window by two corners.

Any iPhone or iPad

Draw the walls

Drag the corners into place and tap any length to type the real figure. Good for a room you measured with a tape.

No camera needed

Check it in 3D

Turn the scan with two fingers, pinch to zoom, see every size with Dims. Keep the furniture or leave it out, then save.

Before you save
The floor plan

Room by room, the home builds itself.

Add the next room and tap the door you walked through: it lands door to door, sharing the wall. Scanned rooms on their own? Connect them. Add a floor for the basement or upstairs. One scan that caught two rooms? Split it.

Add the next room

Tap Add Room, pick the room, then tap the door you walk through. It lands door to door and shares the wall.

Connect what you scanned apart

On the Floor Plan, pick a room, tap Connect, then the same door in each. Long press a room to move it; it snaps to its neighbour.

Two rooms in one scan? Split it.

When the app says Looks Like 2 Rooms, tap Split and name each part. Each gets its own area.

Add a floor

Tap + Floor for the basement or upstairs. Each floor has its own plan, and the PDF keeps them in order.

In 3D

Turn it around.

Every room comes back as a model you can turn and zoom, walls fading as you look through them, the furniture where it stood. The whole home together, or one room at a time.

Wall views

Every wall, flat.

Tap a wall on the plan to see it face-on with its doors and windows and its net square feet. Add an opening and drag it into place; tap any length to type the real one. Each room's page adds it all up: floor, ceiling, walls net and gross, perimeter and height.

Tap a wall

On the room's plan, tap a wall to see it flat. Edit, then Add, puts in a door, window or opening; drag it into place.

Every figure, checked

Floor, ceiling, walls net and gross, perimeter and ceiling height, with the doors and windows counted. Never a guessed area.

The PDF

A clean page. Nothing else on it.

The Home Inspect logo and your project's name at the top, then the drawing. The whole home's plan first, then each room with its walls, lengths, doors and windows, its name and area. Add a room's wall views if you want them. Pick the rooms, tap Share.

Private by design

Yours, and only yours.

No account to make, no server of ours, nothing to track. Your projects live on your device and in your own iCloud, where only you can read them.

Your own iCloud

Projects live on your iPhone and in your private iCloud, so they show up on your iPad and come back if you reinstall. Turn Back up to iCloud off and they stay on the phone alone.

No account

No sign-up and no password to remember. Open the app, name a project and start measuring.

A camera that only measures

The camera runs only while you scan a room, to find its walls. Nothing it sees is saved or sent anywhere, and the app takes no photos.

Nothing collected

No analytics, no ads, no tracking, no address, no third-party anything. Apple handles the payment. The privacy policy says it all in plain words.
