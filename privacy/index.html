Privacy Policy

Effective November 18, 2026

Home Inspect: Room Scanner is an independently developed iPhone and iPad app for measuring the rooms of a home and drawing their floor plans. It is built so that your work stays yours: the app has no servers of its own and no account to make, and nothing you draw is ever sent to us.

Home Inspect: Room Scanner is made and operated by Jason Crane, an individual developer in the United States, reachable at roomscanner@3dlibrary.app. For the purposes of the GDPR and similar laws, that is the data controller for the small amount of information described below.

What the app stores, and where

  • Your projects: the rooms you scan or draw, with their walls, doors, windows and furniture, the floor plans, the wall views, your notes and each project's name, are stored on your device in the app's own private storage, which iOS encrypts whenever the device is locked.
  • Your iCloud: the same projects are kept in your own private iCloud database, tied to your Apple Account, so they appear on your other Apple devices and come back if you reinstall the app. Apple encrypts them in transit and at rest. Nobody else can read them, including us. They count against your iCloud storage. You can turn this off with Back up to iCloud in the app's Settings (your projects then stay only on the device, and turning it back on catches iCloud up), by signing out of iCloud, or by turning the app off under iCloud in the device's Settings.
  • Settings: the app's own preferences, such as its light or dark look and feet or meters, are stored on your device.

What leaves your device

  • Your projects, to your own iCloud, as described above.
  • Whatever you choose to share: when you share a PDF or a drawing, it goes to the app or person you pick in the share sheet, and nowhere else.
  • Your purchase: subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details.

That is the whole list. The app never asks for a home's address, uses no maps, and shares nothing with any company, group or other person.

The camera

The camera is used only while you scan a room, with the LiDAR scanner (Detect Walls) or by marking its corners (Detect Corners), to find the walls, doors and windows. A scan keeps no video, photo or picture of any kind; only the measurements are saved. The app takes no photos and never reads your photo library. iOS asks your permission before the first scan, and you can take it back under the app in the device's Settings; you can still draw walls by hand.

What we do not do

  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking of any kind.
  • No third-party software development kits, and nothing loaded from anyone else's servers. This website is the same: no cookies, no trackers, no outside scripts.
  • No account, no email address, no password to create.

Deleting your data

Delete All My Data in the app's Settings removes every project from your device and from your iCloud. Deleting the app removes the copy on the device; your iCloud copy stays until you remove it, in the app or under iCloud storage in the device's Settings.

Children

Home Inspect: Room Scanner is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them. It collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
